Ask the map

Plain-English search over AlphaEarth satellite embeddings, OpenStreetMap and NASA POWER climate, in four geographical study areas.

Just a quick demo to explore using satellite embeddings with climate data for natural language queries. Be gentle, it's not production-grade, but it could be with more effort 🙂

Data: AlphaEarth Foundations Satellite Embedding (Google and Google DeepMind), USDA NASS Cropland Data Layer, USFWS National Wetlands Inventory, USGS/LBNL US Large-Scale Solar PV Database, NASA POWER (NASA Langley Research Center), © OpenStreetMap contributors. Imagery: USGS The National Map. Language model: openai/gpt-oss-120b (open weights) via Hugging Face. Answers come from automated maps and can be wrong.
